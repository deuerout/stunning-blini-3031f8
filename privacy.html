Privacy

Privacy notice.

How Deverout and Associates collects, uses and protects the personal information you give us through this website, including the Supply Ready Jamaica tool.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who we are

Deverout and Associates is a business transformation consultancy based in Bedford, Bedfordshire and London, England. We decide how and why your personal information is used, which makes us the data controller under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and, where you are in Jamaica, the Jamaica Data Protection Act, 2020.

Contact us about privacy at deverout@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you save results in Supply Ready Jamaica

  • Your name, business name and email address
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number, if you give it
  • Your parish, main target market and what you supply
  • Your readiness answers, your scores and the gaps they show
  • Your agreement to be contacted

When you use our contact form

  • Your name, email address and organisation
  • Your area of interest and the message you write

What stays on your own device

Supply Ready Jamaica remembers your answers and whether you have unlocked your plan in your browser’s local storage, so your progress is still there when you come back. This stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you submit the sign-up form. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website.

How we use it

  • To show you your full action plan
  • To contact you about your results and how we could help your business prepare for government and tourism contracts
  • To reply to enquiries sent through our contact form
  • To understand, in general terms, where Jamaican small businesses most need support, so we can improve our advice and this tool

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with the Jamaica Business Development Corporation, the Government of Jamaica, hotels or any other buyer. Supply Ready Jamaica is an independent tool and is not affiliated with those organisations.

Our lawful basis

We rely on your consent, given when you tick the box on the sign-up form, to contact you about your results. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us, and we will stop contacting you. For replies to contact-form enquiries we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to people who get in touch.

Who processes it for us

Form submissions are received and stored by Netlify, Inc., which hosts this website, and are forwarded to our email inbox. Netlify is based in the United States, so your information may be transferred outside the UK and Jamaica. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards our providers offer under UK data protection law, such as standard contractual clauses. Fonts on this website load from Google Fonts, which receives your device’s IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep sign-up and enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them, unless you become a client, in which case our client records policy applies. You can ask us to delete your details sooner.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or complete it
  • ask us to delete it
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it
  • withdraw your consent to being contacted
  • receive your information in a portable format

Email deverout@gmail.com to use any of these rights. We will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to a regulator:

  • In Jamaica: the Office of the Information Commissioner
  • In the United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk)

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if how we use personal information changes, and change the date at the top.

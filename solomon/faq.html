Q.

Why does the Solomon site use the domain deuerout.com when the company is called Deverout and Associates?

The spelling difference between the company name, Deverout and Associates, and the domain, deuerout.com, is intentional, not an error and not a separate or unaffiliated entity. All Solomon licensing, billing and compliance communications — including this FAQ, the compliance kit, and account emails — operate from deuerout.com and @deuerout.com addresses as the authoritative source for Deverout and Associates.

If you receive a communication claiming to be from Deverout and Associates on a domain other than deuerout.com, treat it as unverified and confirm through our contact page before acting on it.

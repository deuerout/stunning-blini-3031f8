## London

- Status: real office. Address locality London, GB.
- Economy to verify: City and Canary Wharf financial services, fintech, insurance (Lloyd's market), NHS trusts and life sciences (Knowledge Quarter, White City), creative and professional services.
- Culture: global city; large Caribbean, African, South Asian and European diasporas; Windrush heritage links to the Kingston formation in the voice guide. City workforce is among the youngest, most highly skilled and most international in the country (City of London Corporation, City Statistics briefing).
- Search phrases: business transformation consultancy London; AI transformation consultant London; AI governance consulting financial services; digital transformation healthcare London.

      About & Mission

      Analytical truth, in realtime, for everyone

      We are building the connective tissue between blockchain transparency and operational cyber defence.

      The Global Shift began with a simple observation: blockchains publish everything and explain nothing. Trillions in value move across public ledgers every year, yet the people who need to understand that movement — analysts, compliance teams, security engineers — are still stitching together spreadsheets and screenshots.

      Hermes is our answer. It is an agentic AI analyst that reads the chain the way a human investigator would: forming a hypothesis, pulling evidence, cross-referencing threat feeds, and reporting back with its confidence clearly stated. It does not guess. When the data is thin, it says so.

      Mythos supplies the adversary view. Exploit signatures, mixer fingerprints, sanctioned counterparties and emerging attack patterns are continuously matched against live on-chain behaviour — so a wallet is never just an address, it is a risk profile with a history.

      Our mission: make blockchain intelligence as legible as a weather report — realtime, verifiable, and available to anyone defending value on-chain.
